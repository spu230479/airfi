How you will get the refund

Banking rules require us to send your refund to the card you used to pay your last Self Assessment bill.

Sometimes we cannot do this, for example if your card has expired or been cancelled.

To make sure we can pay your refund, we'll ask you to enter your bank account details. You will not need to sign into your bank.
