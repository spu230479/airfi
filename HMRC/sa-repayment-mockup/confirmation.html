What happens next

We will try to send your refund to the card you used to pay your last Self Assessment bill. If we cannot do this, for example because your card has expired or been cancelled, we will pay it into bank account ending 678.

You should get the refund by 8 February 2027. To protect you against fraud your refund can sometimes take longer.

You can check the status of your refund in your HMRC online account.

If you have not received your refund by 8 February 2027, you can contact us. Do not contact us before this date as we will not have an update for you.
