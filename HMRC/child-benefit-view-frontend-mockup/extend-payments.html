Extend your Child Benefit payments while a young person stays in education

Use this form if they will be studying:

  • GCSEs or A levels
  • BTECs, level 1, 2 or 3
  • T levels
  • NVQs, level 1, 2 or 3
  • International Baccalaureate
  • Scottish Highers up to and including level 7

They must also be:

  • 16 or 17 on 1 September 2026
  • on their course for 1 or 2 academic years
  • studying full-time, which means more than 12 hours a week
  • on a course provided by a school or college
  • living with you in the UK
